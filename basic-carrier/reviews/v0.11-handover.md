# v0.11 handover for review

> Kept as the record of the v0.11 design review. Paths are those of the BasicCarrier repository at the time: figures are now next to this file, the re-review's copper model is in `tools/analysis/`, and the other scripts it mentions are in the git history (before the `reviews/` clean-up).

A plain list of what changed between the `v0.10` tag (as built by PCBWay) and the current master, and where to look.
It does not judge whether the changes are correct; that is the reviewer's job.
Coordinates are KiCad page mm. Front is the J2/J19 motor-pad edge (low y), back is the XT90 edge (high y).

## Board outline and mechanics
- Outline was 100 x 90 mm. It is now **91.14 x 90 mm**: x 100.11..191.25, y 50..140.
- Corner mounting holes (3.0 mm) are at (102.61, 52.5), (188.75, 52.5), (102.61, 137.5) and (188.75, 137.5), an **86.14 x 85 mm** pattern.
  They moved twice, in commits 106b853 and 8fac42d.
- Cube 30x30 holes (1.8 mm) at (130/160, 80/110) are unchanged.
- **CubeOrange body outline** (assumed 38.25 mm square on the Cube holes) is drawn on User.Drawings with a note.
  Only J1 is inside it on the top side.
- **XT90 J18** (bottom side) is at (145, 131). Its body sticks out 5 mm past the back edge (commit 8f20da8).
  Pads are at y 122.1 and slots at y 131.
- **J1** is now the DF17(1.0H)-80DP-0.5V(57) plug (4 mm stack). Commits 6e34afd and 5566dce; keepout change in 183ac4b.

## Schematic changes (since v0.10)
- ESC CAN_H/CAN_L swap fixed in the top-level schematic. Commits 3f13c65 and 77dfc9b.
- Fan supply moved to VBATT. FET gate pulldowns and a buzzer discharge resistor added. Commit dd086d2.
- Battery connector J18 changed to XT90PW-M30.G.Y, mounted on the bottom side. Commit 1842516.
- USB/power changes:
  - VBUS detect via U22 (TCM809JENB713, 4.00 V), POW_SEL bulk caps, changeover. Commits 9ea6c8f and efda927.
  - U21 TPS2121 current-limit resistor R134 set to 56.2k. Commits e53f154 and f470bce.
- Smaller buck input capacitors. Commit 4ff8767.
- Net renames FAN_SW_GND / BUZZ_SW_GND. Commits 94fd818, 6a7d32d and d87c4e3.
- ERC clean-up and PWR_FLAGs. Commits f425870 and 96ace71.
- Not changed in the shrink/label work: the schematic. The netlist checker (`tools/check_netlist.py`) gives 0 errors and 0 warnings;
  report in `review/v0.11-netlist-report.md`.

## Layout changes, by area

### Centre back (XT90 / battery input)
- **J18 XT90**: moved, see above.
- **D1** (SMCJ33A TVS): placed vertically on the top side between the XT90 pads, at (145, 122.5).
- **C112** (330 uF): moved to the top side at (145, 132.9).
- **F1**: on the bottom at (146.5, 111.9). The 2.25 mm track to C111 was re-routed.
- **GND/VBATT connector pours (F+B)**: reshaped around D1 and F1. Extra GND/VBATT stitching vias were added where the old XT90 pads were.
- **In3 VBATT feed to the FAN connector J14**: re-routed around the XT90 GND pad.
- **Parts moved off the XT90 body (bottom side)**:
  - U15 (CAN2 transceiver) moved +1.4 mm, with its local fan-out hand-routed by script. R90 and C83 were re-placed.
  - R117 turned vertical next to U19. The CAN_H_2 and CAN_L_2 vias near it were moved.
  - D15 nudged.

### All four ESC power stages
- MOSFET columns and motor pads went from about 10 mm to **9.4 mm pitch**; the inner column did not move.
- Parts outboard of the inner column shifted with the board edge: left +1.11 mm, right -1.75 mm.
- Motor pads (J2/J19/J22/J25, top side) moved with the MOSFET columns.
- Routing squeezed by this was repaired by script (`review/v0.11-shrink2/scripts/`):
  - Clashing stitching vias removed.
  - About 100 short track gaps re-routed.
  - FD6288 driver corners re-routed: U3 (HO1, VB1, VS1) and U12 (HO3, VB3, VS2).
  - GND fill islands re-stitched.
  - Three tight GND via columns between the back-right phases thinned. They had split the rear-right VBATT pour.
  - Extra VBATT vias added to the ESC VBATT pours (VBATT_ESC..ESC3), and between F.Cu and In2/In3 on the right side.
- **Side VBATT strips** (F/In2/In3): extra In2/In3 VBATT pours added at the inner side of each strip.
  Zones named VBATT_STRIP_WIDEN, at about x 106..109 and 180..183 after the shift.
- **ESC3 VBATT pour (front-left)**: vias added under RSHUNT5.

### LED / FMU connectors (top side, same layout as SmallUAV06)
| Position | Ref | Net | Spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| front-right | J4 | FMU_CH1 | (183.5, 67.3) |
| back-right | J6 | FMU_CH2 | (184.0, 122.6) |
| back-left | J7 | FMU_CH3 | (105.9, 122.6) |
| front-left | J15 | FMU_CH4 | (105.0, 67.4) |
| back | J16 | FMU_CH5 | (131.5, 125.3) |

- The corner spots sit above the MOSFET rows, on MVDD/VBATT/GND pours.
- PWM and +5.2V were re-routed by script from their old positions in the band between the ESCs.
- Each GND pad has its own GND via.
- Stitching vias that were under the connectors were moved next to them.
- J7 was later moved under the back-left ESC capacitor block (mirror of J6), by `scripts/move_j7.py`:
  - FMU_CH3 and +5.2V hop under the ESC3 HO1 gate track on B.Cu; +5.2V joins the In2 +5.2V track in the GND plane, and the old right-hand +5.2V feed is tied to via (119.7, 118.14) on F.Cu.
  - The ESC3 FD6288_VS1 track (F.Cu) ran through the new pad 4 spot. It now dog-legs at x 110.45, right of J7.
  - J7 mounting pad 4 is tied to pad 5 (GND via) by a F.Cu track under the connector body.
  - Two MVDD stitching vias under the GND pad were removed; one was put back under the connector body.
- J6 was then moved 1.5 mm inboard (courtyard 3.35 mm from the right edge; J4 is 3.85 mm), by `scripts/move_j6.py`:
  - The ESC2 FD6288_LO1 track (F.Cu) was straightened between the MVDD vias and J6 pad 5.
  - Five VBATT vias and one GND via that hit the new pads were moved next to them; pad 3 got a new GND via.
  - A GND via was added at (188.58, 125.97) to keep the B.Cu GND area below J6 connected.
  - It could not go the full 2 mm to match J4 without moving MVDD stitching vias and LO1 further.

### Silkscreen
- Labels next to the JST-GH connectors are 2x (some 1.75x) and nudged up to 1.6 mm.
  SERIAL 2, Remote ID, USBC, FAN and FAN's V mark were left at the original size.
- CH1..CH5 added next to the LED connectors.
- "BasicCarrier ${REVISION}" (shows v0.11) at about (164.7, 134), right of C112.
- CAN_1 -> CAN1 (J10 and J11, both CAN1 nets).
- Serial1 (2.0 mm, above J12). Serial2 is now 2.0 mm and horizontal above J13, like Serial1; there was no room beside J13.
- FAN -> Fan, 2.0 mm, right of J14. BUZ -> Buzzer (2.0 mm). Script: `scripts/silk2.py`.
- ESC_DBG labels -> ESC1 DBG (front-right), ESC2 DBG (back-right), ESC3 DBG (back-left), ESC4 DBG (front-left).
- RC label is 2.0 mm; the J3 refdes moved to the top-left of J3. Script: `scripts/silk.py`.
- The old LED/V/G labels in the mid band were removed. "space for SD card" and the User.Comments notes are unchanged.
