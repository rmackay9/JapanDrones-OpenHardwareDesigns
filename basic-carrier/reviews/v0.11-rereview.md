# BasicCarrier v0.11: independent re-review after the layout fixes

> Kept as the record of the v0.11 design review. Paths are those of the BasicCarrier repository at the time: figures are now next to this file, the re-review's copper model is in `tools/analysis/`, and the other scripts it mentions are in the git history (before the `reviews/` clean-up).

Date: 2026-11-27. Reviewed state: commit `ec7e72b` on `master` (fix commits `0da7e29`..`ec7e72b` on top of the reviewed `c4ec655`).
Read-only review. No design file was changed. All analysis ran on copies outside the repository.

**Verdict: the battery path is fixed, but do not order yet.**
B1, M1 and M6 are fixed and my numbers agree with the fixer's to within 8 %.
B2 is fixed as it was written (one piece of copper per layer, 30–38 vias per ESC), but one of the later via moves (M4, commit `32b3a7c`) pinched ESC2's MVDD copper down to a 0.42 mm bridge on each outer layer at the same spot, and everything ESC2 draws goes through those two bridges (R1).
That is a ten-minute fix. The other open decision is the three FD6288 pins that still have an open via under the lead (R2).

## How this was checked

- Nothing from `review/v0.11-fixes/analysis/` was run or reused. I wrote a separate dump and model (`review/v0.11-rereview/scripts/`, now `tools/analysis/`).
- The board was dumped read-only with KiCad 10.0.8's `pcbnew` Python: every pad, track, via and filled zone polygon.
  The pre-fix board (`c4ec655`) and the `v0.10` tag were dumped the same way for comparison.
- Copper was rasterised at 0.0125–0.02 mm and islands labelled per net and layer. Connectivity through vias was followed across all six layers.
- Current paths were solved on a resistor grid (0.1 mm cells, 0.04 mm at the necks) with conjugate gradients: 1 oz (35 µm) copper on every layer as in the board stackup, 20 µm via plating, copper at 20 °C. DC only, no thermal model. Treat the numbers as ±20 %; hot copper adds about 25 %.
- As a check on the model, the pre-fix board gives 989 mV and 1005 mV to the two left shunts and 49.8 W of VBATT copper loss, against 1.00 V, 1.02 V and 51 W in the first review.
- Narrow passages were measured from the filled copper itself (largest circle that can pass from the capacitor strip to the copper over each FET), not read off the solver.
- `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all` on a copy, with and without `--refill-zones`: the committed fills are current (areas identical after refill).
- The two new DRC rules were tested on a scratch copy by adding deliberate violations.
- `tools/check_netlist.py` was run against the project schematic and the pin-mapping spreadsheet.

Figures are in `reviews/v0.11-rereview/`.

## Major

### R1. ESC2: all MVDD current passes two 0.42 mm copper bridges (new, from the M4 via move in `32b3a7c`)

**Where:** F.Cu and B.Cu at (182.0, 118.6), the corner where ESC2's capacitor strip turns into the FET row, just above J6.
Figures: `esc2-neck-F.png`, `esc2-neck-B.png`, `esc2-neck-B-before-M4.png`, `mvdd-current-density.png` (second row).

**What is wrong.** ESC2's MVDD pour is L-shaped: the shunt and the eight 10 µF capacitors sit in a 4.8 mm strip (x 181–185.8), the three high-side FETs sit along a bar (y 119.5–125).
On both outer layers the strip joins the bar only through the gap between two via clearances:
- the `FD6288_VB3` via at (181.38, 117.91), and
- the J6 pin 3 GND via at (182.75, 119.25).

The gap is 0.42 mm on F.Cu and 0.42 mm on B.Cu, one above the other. The rest of the strip's width is blocked by J6's pads on top and by the J6 GND and `FMU_CH2_PROT` vias on the bottom.
The 30 MVDD vias do not help, because both layers neck at the same place and there is no MVDD copper on the inner layers.

The VB3 via used to sit at (181.38, 116.26). Only its land touched D11's pad, by 0.03 mm, and the land is tented, so the hole was never in the pad and it did not need to move.
The M4 pass moved it 1.65 mm south with a stub on each layer, straight into this corner.
Before that move the passage was 0.96 mm on B.Cu. For comparison v0.10 had about 1.8–2.4 mm on each layer.

**Evidence** (0.04 mm grid, 20 A from the shunt to Q17):

| | F.Cu | B.Cu |
|---|---|---|
| Passage width | 0.42 mm | 0.42 mm |
| Current through it | 10.4 A | 9.6 A |
| Peak sheet current | 33 A/mm (about 930 A/mm² in 35 µm copper) | 26 A/mm |
| Loss within 1 mm of the bridge | 135 mW | 133 mW |

About 0.34 W is dissipated within 1.5 mm of that point. The bridges are short, so they are heat-sunk by the copper on both sides: my estimate from copper conduction alone is 10–15 °C above the surrounding board at 20 A continuous and four times that at 40 A. That is an estimate, not a simulation.
It will not fuse at rated current, but it is the single path for one ESC, it has no margin for etch tolerance (0.42 mm ± 20 %), and it also carries the ripple current between the capacitors and the FETs. It is the same class of problem as the original B2 and it is cheap to remove.

**Fix:**
1. Take the VB3 via at (181.38, 117.91) and its two 1.65 mm stubs out of the corner. The simplest way is to put the via back at (181.38, 116.26), where only its tented land brushed D11's pad.
   Or delete it and join the F.Cu track end at (181.38, 116.26) to the existing VB3 via at (181.20, 116.85), which already carries the B.Cu side, with a 0.6 mm F.Cu stub (clearance there not checked).
   I removed the via and stubs on a scratch copy and refilled: the passage goes back to **0.96 mm per layer** and the drop to Q19 falls from 58 to 52 mV.
2. To get more than that, move the J6 pin 3 GND via at (182.75, 119.25) about 0.6 mm east, or pull the `FD6288_LO1` track at x = 180.4 west so the zone's inside corner at (181.01, 119.5) can move with it. Aim for 1.5 mm or more on each layer.
3. Re-check with `scripts/neck.py`, which prints the narrowest passage per ESC and layer.

### R2. Open vias under three FD6288 pins (M4 leftover)

**Where** (B.Cu, QFN-24 pads 0.25 × 0.82 mm, pitch 0.5 mm):

| Pin | Net | Via | Where the hole sits | If the joint is open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U3.15 | ESC1 `FD6288_VS2` | 0.20 / 0.40 at (177.50, 76.90) | On the pad centre line, under the inner end of the lead | Phase-2 high-side driver loses its reference. The gate of Q11 is then undefined: the worst of the three. |
| U3.13 | ESC1 `FD6288_HO3` | 0.25 / 0.45 at (177.725, 75.80) | Half on the pad (0.1 mm off the centre line), under the lead | Q13 never turns on. ESC1 runs on two phases or faults. |
| U12.9 | ESC2 `FD6288_LO3` | 0.20 / 0.40 at (177.64, 112.70) | On the centre line, under the lead | Q20 never turns on. Same symptom on ESC2. |

**Risk.** All three holes are under the package body (body edge 2.0 mm from the centre; the holes are at 1.69–1.92 mm), directly beneath the lead.
A 0.25 mm pad with a 0.1 mm stencil gets roughly 0.010 mm³ of solder. The barrel of a 0.2 mm via holds 0.05 mm³, five times that.
The far end is tented, which limits how much solder runs in, but it also traps flux gas under the joint.
v0.10's one case (U2.19) is not a guide: that via sat at the toe of the pad, outside the body.
I rate each joint as a real chance of a starved or open connection, not a certainty. The trouble is that a weak joint can pass a bench test and open later with vibration.

**Can they be moved?** I searched for myself, with a maze search on B.Cu from each pad to any spot where a 0.40 / 0.20 via clears every other net on all six layers at the board's 0.152 mm rule, plus the jog on the other layer:
- U12.9: no position within 4 mm. Confirmed boxed in (C75.2 on top, pins 8 and 10, `FD6288_VS2`).
- U3.15: no position within 4 mm. Confirmed.
- U3.13: one pocket of 0.03 mm² near (177.62, 75.51), 0.3 mm from the old via. Too small to rely on.

So the fixer's "boxed in" is right. Moving them means re-placing C54, C59 and C75 and re-routing those corners.

**Cleanest fix, in order of preference:**
1. Order the board with resin-filled and capped via-in-pad (IPC-4761 type VII) from PCBWay. No layout change and it also covers C43.1, U20.2 and the connector tabs. It applies to the whole board and costs extra; ask for the price with the quote.
2. If not, enlarge the paste aperture on these three pads outward by about 0.3 mm (pad-level paste setting, no component change), tell PCBWay which three pins to inspect, and spin every motor on ESC1 and ESC2 under load before the boards are used. That screens open joints but not marginal ones.
3. Re-place those two driver corners in the next revision.

## Minor

### R3. Left VBATT strip: no top copper at all and a 5.6 mm neck for 40 A (B1 residual, partly new)

**Where:** left edge, y 106–125. Figures `left-strip-In2.png`, `left-strip-In3.png`.

- The F.Cu strip that was 0.3–0.9 mm wide is now 0 mm wide between y = 106.2 and 125: the M6 pull-back removed what was left. The fixer's note says it is "not 2.5 mm wide"; it is gone. Both left ESCs (40 A) are fed on In2 and In3 only.
- Narrowest total cross-section: **5.56 mm at y = 124.3** (In2 4.26 mm, In3 1.30 mm), then 8.64 mm at y = 118.8. Elsewhere 11–17 mm. The right side never drops below 15.1 mm and keeps 1.2–3.6 mm of top copper all the way.
- The y = 124.3 neck is made by the new In3 `FD6288_HO1` track from (109.75, 123.95) to its via at (105.53, 124.00), which slots the In3 strip for 4 mm, in line with the GND via at (101.60, 124.31) and the MVDD via at (104.41, 124.40).
- Solver at 40 A: 9.7 A/mm mean and 16 A/mm peak on In3 in the neck, 0.31 W between y = 123 and 125, 1.73 W over the whole strip from y = 105 to 125 (0.83 W per cm² of board, the densest region on the board; the right strip is 0.44 W/cm²).

It works: the drop is 137–149 mV. It is the weakest part of the battery path that is left.

**Fix:** move the HO1 via from (105.53, 124.00) to the east edge of the strip, about (109.3, 124.0), and run the last 4 mm on B.Cu, so In3 is not slotted. Move the GND via at (101.60, 124.31) 2 mm north or south so it is not in line with the others. That should take the neck to roughly 10 mm (not tried).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESC1 | 0.52 mm at (181.4, 66.7) | 3.20 mm | 3.7 mm | 49 mV |
| ESC2 | 0.42 mm | 0.42 mm | **0.84 mm** (R1) | 58 mV |

ESC3 is the next weakest: 8.5 A crosses the F.Cu passage and 12.4 A the B.Cu one, with 15 and 13 A/mm peaks. Its F.Cu passage is between the J7 +5.2V via at (107.11, 118.60) and C102's GND pad; the B.Cu one was already 1.48 mm before the fixes.

With 0.25 mm on those zones (tried on a scratch copy) the passages become ESC1 1.00, ESC2 0.91, ESC3 0.96 and 1.78, ESC4 1.00 mm, the left-strip neck of R3 goes from 5.6 to 8.8 mm, and VBATT loss falls from 10.1 to 6.6 W.
That change is not free: on my copy three small GND fill pieces lost their connection and need a stitching via each, so it needs its own DRC pass.

### R5. MVDD current is shared by a handful of vias, not 30–38

The new via blocks sit at the FET end of each pour. The old vias between the capacitor columns, nearest the shunt, still take most of the transfer from top to bottom:

| | Vias | Carrying more than 0.5 A | Busiest via | Top five together |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESC1 | 35 | 7–12 | 4.4 A, 0.25 mm drill, (183.35, 79.70) | 16.4 A |
| ESC2 | 30 | 11–13 | 4.1 A, 0.25 mm, (183.36, 110.57) | 12.4 A |
| ESC3 | 38 | 8–15 | 4.3 A, 0.25 mm, (106.01, 109.90) | 13.5 A |
| ESC4 | 37 | 12–21 | 4.6 A, 0.30 mm, (106.01, 79.70) | 12.2 A |

The same on the VBATT side of two shunts: ESC2's shunt pad copper has 10 vias for its 20 A (3.3 A in the busiest), ESC4's has 16 (3.65 A).
I do not think this is dangerous: 4.4 A in a 0.25 mm via with 20 µm plating is 30 mW, which is about 1 °C of self-heating when both ends sit in solid copper, and losing one via just moves the load to its neighbours.
It is well above the 1–2 A per via the first review used as its yardstick, so it is worth stating.

**Fix:** where there is room, add 4–6 vias of 0.3 mm within 3 mm of each shunt's MVDD pad and of the ESC2 and ESC4 VBATT shunt pads.

### R7. M1 residual: about 10 W in the battery copper at full rated current

At 4 × 20 A the VBATT copper dissipates 10.1 W with copper at 20 °C, about 12.5 W at 80 °C. In2 and In3 take 3.9 W each, the top layer 2.1 W.
By region: rear band left of the XT90 2.6 W, right 1.9 W, left strip 2.1 W, right strip 1.7 W, the two rear corners 1.0 W.
Add about 3.5 W for the four MVDD pours, 1.3 W for GND and 1.6 W for the shunts.
At hover (5 A per ESC) the VBATT figure is 0.63 W, which is nothing.
That is a fifth of the 49.8 W before the fixes and half of the 20 W the fixer's model gives for v0.10 (I did not solve v0.10 myself).
I have no further layout change to ask for beyond R3 and R4; just do not expect 80 A continuous without airflow over the board.

- **Motor pads J22 and J25:** the vias moved from y = 139.20 to 139.17 for M6. They are wire-solder pads; no concern.

## Nit

1. **Longer gate runs.** Driver pin to gate resistor: ESC1 HO3 16.4 → 22.0 mm, ESC2 HO2 16.1 → 25.8 mm, ESC2 LO1 21.4 → 23.7 mm, ESC3 HO1 16.2 → 18.3 mm, ESC4 HO1 12.6 → 14.4 mm, ESC4 HO2 20.3 → 25.7 mm and now with six layer changes instead of two. All are shorter than the longest run that was already there (ESC2 HO3, 32.9 mm), and with 10 Ω gate resistors a few nH more does not matter. ESC4 HO2 would be tidier on one inner layer.
2. **Dangling via.** `+5.2V` via at (124.85, 73.95) is now connected on In3 only (DRC `via_dangling`). Delete it.
3. **LED supply resistance.** The `+5.2V` re-routes add 22 mΩ to J15 (219 → 241 mΩ, 176 mm of 0.3–0.4 mm track) and 17 mΩ to J16 (172 → 189 mΩ). About 0.24 V at 1 A.
4. **XT90 + pin** is now joined solidly to four copper layers (F, In2, In3, B) instead of two. It will need as much heat as the − pin, which PCBWay managed on v0.10.
5. **Motor pads** come to 0.39 mm from the rear edge on F.Cu. The M6 rule exempts pads; the pour behind them is at 0.51 mm.
6. **The DRU file is sound**, with three things to know: the `CORNER_KO` areas are 36-sided polygons of 3.46 mm radius (3.45 mm across the flats), not 3.5 mm; the corner rule covers the outer layers only and exempts pads; the edge rule covers only `VBATT`, `*/MVDD` and `*/FD6288_VS*`, so other nets still use the board's 0.2 mm.
7. **Not new, seen in passing:** a `+5.2V` F.Cu track runs 0.35 mm from the wall of the Cube mounting hole at (130, 80), on the side the Cube sits on. It was there before the fixes.
8. **Hairline zone joint.** DRC reports a 0.002 mm connection between `VBATT_STRIP_WIDEN` and `VBATT_REAR_INNER` on In2. Both zones are joined elsewhere; extend one outline by 0.1 mm to silence it.

## Checked and OK

**Battery path**
- Rear band, both sides of the XT90: no cross-section below 10.1 mm of copper (x = 176.5) and 13.0 mm (x = 129.6); typical 20–35 mm. Mean sheet current 1–2.5 A/mm, short peaks of 4–4.5 A/mm on F.Cu between the motor-pad via groups.
- Right strip: 15.1 mm minimum for 40 A, 11.1 mm where it carries 20 A.
- VBATT vias: 212, highest current 3.65 A, five above 3 A, 0.15 W in all barrels together.
- In2 and In3 share evenly (3.86 and 3.87 W). The top layer carries 9 A of the left 40 A and 16 A of the right.

**What the fixes moved**
- Tracks moved to In2/In3 (`FMU_CH1/4/5_PROT`, `+5.2V`, HO/LO/VS): every net is continuous (0 unconnected). Largest new broadside overlaps across the 0.1 mm In2–In3 prepreg: `+5.2V` over ESC3 `LO3` 1.25 mm² (about 0.5 pF), `FMU_CH5` over `FMU_CH3` 0.64 mm², ESC2 `LO1` over its own `VS1` 0.38 mm², ESC4 `HO2` over `FMU_CH4` 0.24 mm². All are a fraction of a picofarad into low-impedance nodes. `FMU_CH5_PROT` now also crosses the four CAN lines (0.14–0.18 mm² each). No high-impedance sense net (INA180 outputs, BEMF dividers, battery-voltage ADC) gained an overlap.
- Gate tracks in the rear band now face VBATT instead of GND on the neighbouring inner layer (for example ESC3 `LO1` 73 % GND → 37 % GND, 31 % VBATT). In1 and In4 are still solid above and below, so the return path is not broken.
- C21 and D2 near U2: both 0.25 mm further from the driver. The HO1 via is at (114.40, 74.28), 0.32 mm clear of pin 19's pad. VB1 path 3.7 → 3.8 mm. No courtyard or clearance issue.
