# BasicCarrier v0.11: independent pre-fabrication review

> Kept as the record of the v0.11 design review. Paths are those of the BasicCarrier repository at the time: figures are now next to this file, the re-review's copper model is in `tools/analysis/`, and the other scripts it mentions are in the git history (before the `reviews/` clean-up).

Date: 2026-11-27. Reviewed state: commit `3f088f2` ("PCB: Randy's silkscreen label adjustments") on `master`.
Read-only review. No design file was changed.

**Verdict: do not send to PCBWay yet.** Two layout regressions from this week's shrink and connector moves choke the battery current:
B1 leaves the two left-hand ESCs fed through a 0.3–0.9 mm sliver of copper, and B2 leaves ESC1 fed through a single via and most of ESC2 through two.
Six further items are likely to cause field or assembly problems and are cheap to fix now.

| Severity | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Blocker | 2 | Board won't work or will be damaged |
| Major | 6 | Likely field problem or rework |
| Minor | 19 | Should fix, low risk if not |
| Nit | 10 | Cosmetic or housekeeping |

Coordinates are KiCad page mm. Front is the low-y edge (J2/J19), back is the XT90 edge (y = 140).
ESC numbering follows the silkscreen: ESC1 front-right (sheet `VimDrones_mod1`), ESC2 back-right (`mod2`), ESC3 back-left (`mod3`), ESC4 front-left (`mod`).

## How this was checked

- `kicad-cli` 10.0.8: DRC with schematic parity (0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 parity, 483 warnings), ERC (0 errors, 152 library-link warnings), netlist export.
- The board was loaded read-only with KiCad's `pcbnew` Python module and every pad, track, via and filled zone dumped for analysis.
  The `v0.10` tag was dumped the same way, so each finding is marked as new in v0.11 or carried over.
- Copper current paths were solved numerically: a 0.2 mm resistor grid over all six layers, vias as vertical links, 1 oz (35 µm) copper on every layer as in the board stackup, 20 µm via plating.
  It is a DC model with no thermal part. Treat the numbers as ±20 %.
  Where the model pointed at a bottleneck, the copper was re-checked by labelling connected islands of the zone fills at 0.0125 mm resolution.
- Datasheets were read from the manufacturer PDFs listed at the end. Pin tables and land patterns were compared against the netlist and the footprint pad coordinates, not against the symbols alone.
- J1 was compared pin by pin and pad by pad with CubePilot's reference carrier (`CubePilotReference_EAGLE_01100-05120_RevB2`, read only).
- The XT90 was checked against the AMASS 2026 catalogue drawing and against PCBWay's own photo of the v0.10 board.

The KiCad DRC rules in the project are much looser than a fab's (minimum via annular ring 0.008 mm, hole-to-hole 0.1 mm, copper-to-edge 0.2 mm).
"0 DRC errors" therefore says little about manufacturability; the DFM items below were measured separately.

## Blocker

### B1. VBATT feed to ESC3 and ESC4 is a 0.3–0.9 mm sliver of top copper (new in v0.11)

**Where:** F.Cu, left board edge, x 100.31–101.2, y 106–125. Inner layers In2/In3, left strip, x 100–109, y 105–140.

**What is wrong.** Battery current for both left ESCs has to travel from the rear-left top pour up the left edge to the ESC3 shunt (RSHUNT5).
After the shrink, the only copper joining them is this:

| y (mm) | F.Cu VBATT width now | v0.10 |
|---|---|---|
| 107–108 | 0.30 mm | 3.3 mm |
| 109–118 | 0.68–0.87 mm | 3.7 mm |
| 119–122 | 2.7–3.0 mm | 5.8 mm |
| 124–125 | 0.56–0.90 mm | 5.8 mm |

The strip is squeezed between the board edge (copper stops at x = 100.31) and the `MVDD_ESC3` zone, whose outline starts at x = 101.11.

The In2/In3 VBATT strips are still there and are 6–9 mm wide, but they are no longer connected at the back.
In v0.10 six vias along x = 97.2 (y = 105.9, 112.5, 119.1, 125.7, 127.3, 132.2) tied the top pour to those strips.
The left edge moved in by 4.1 mm while the parts moved 1.1 mm, so those vias fell off the board and were not replaced.
Now there is no VBATT via in the left strip below y = 105.5 (the right strip has 25).
The inner strips only join the top copper at the ESC3 end, so they carry current onward to ESC4 but none from the battery.

**Evidence.** Zone fills and via list from the board file; figures in `reviews/v0.11-final-review/`:
`vbatt-F.png` and `vbatt-In2.png` (rear half of the board; red is more than 5 A per mm of copper width, dark blue is copper carrying nothing, white circles are VBATT vias) and `left-edge-F.png` (top copper at the left edge, VBATT in red, ESC3 MVDD in yellow).
Solver result with 20 A into each ESC:

| | XT90 + pin → shunt | of which in the neck |
|---|---|---|
| ESC3 (back-left) | 1.00 V | 0.47 V, about 12 mΩ |
| ESC4 (front-left) | 1.02 V | same neck |
| ESC1, ESC2 (right) | 0.25–0.26 V | none |

The neck alone dissipates about 19 W with 40 A through it, 4.7 W at 20 A and 1.2 W at 10 A, in roughly 10 mm² of copper.
A 0.3 mm × 35 µm trace opens at a few amps (Preece's wire formula gives about 3 A; a trace on FR4 lasts somewhat longer).
Two motors hovering are enough to overheat it. If it opens in flight, both left motors stop.

**Fix (mirror what was done on the right side):**
1. Add 10–15 VBATT vias (0.6/0.3 mm) in the rear-left corner, about x 100.9–105, y 126–134, where the F.Cu rear pour overlaps the In2/In3 strips. B.Cu there is GND fill, so nothing is in the way.
2. Pull the left edge of the `MVDD_ESC3` F.Cu zone in from x = 101.11 to about 103.5, to restore a top strip of at least 2.5 mm (the right side has 2.7 mm).
3. Re-run the fill and check the widths in the table above.

I added eight hypothetical vias at (101.2/102.4, 126.5–131.0) to the model to test fix 1 on its own.
The drop to the left shunts fell from 1.00/1.02 V to 0.42/0.43 V and total VBATT copper loss from 51 W to 27 W at 4 × 20 A.
That is still worse than the right side (0.25 V) because the rear-left top pour is long and single-layer, which is M1.

### B2. ESC1 is fed through one via and two phases of ESC2 through two vias (new in v0.11)

**Where:** MVDD pours (shunt to high-side FET drains). ESC1: via at (183.35, 72.30). ESC2: vias at (177.00, 124.50) and (177.95, 124.50).

**What is wrong.** In v0.10 each ESC's top-layer MVDD pour was one piece of about 200 mm² running from the shunt to above the FETs, stitched to the bottom-layer FET copper by 22–27 vias.
The LED-connector moves and the re-routing put new tracks across those pours on both layers, and the pours are now in pieces:

| ESC | Top copper still joined to the shunt | Vias from it into the copper the FET drains sit on | Tracks that cut the pours |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESC1 (front-right) | 58 mm² | **1** (0.25 mm drill). The other three vias at x = 183.35 land in a 32 mm² bottom island that leads nowhere. | B.Cu `FMU_CH1_PROT`; F.Cu `+5.2V` and `FMU_CH1_PROT` |
| ESC2 (back-right) | 69 mm² | 4 into the island holding Q15. Q17 and Q19 sit on a separate bottom island reached only through **2** vias (0.25 mm). | B.Cu `FD6288_VS1`; F.Cu `FD6288_LO1` |
| ESC3 (back-left) | 57 mm² | 5 (0.25 mm) | F.Cu `+5.2V`, `FD6288_HO1` |
| ESC4 (front-left) | 71 mm² | 8 (0.30 mm) | F.Cu `+5.2V`, GND link |
| all four in v0.10 | about 200 mm² | 22–27 | none |

**Evidence.** Island labelling of the filled zones at 0.0125 mm resolution, with via pads counted as copper (figures `mvdd-esc1-F.png`, `mvdd-esc1-B.png`, `mvdd-esc2-F.png`, `mvdd-esc2-B.png`; each colour is one piece of copper).
Solver, 20 A from the shunt to the middle high-side FET:

| | Drop | Highest current in a single via |
|---|---|---|
| ESC1 | 100 mV | 20.0 A (all of it) |
| ESC2 | 114 mV | 10.9 A and 9.1 A |
| ESC3 | 71 mV | 5.8 A |
| ESC4 | 54 mV | 4.5 A |
| v0.10, any ESC | 31–33 mV | 3.8–4.1 A |

A 0.25 mm via with 20 µm plating has about the copper of a 0.5 mm track and is good for 1–2 A continuous.
The ESC1 via also touches the FET-side pour only by the edge of its pad.
If it opens, ESC1's motor stops; if either ESC2 via opens, the other takes the whole load.

**Fix:**
1. ESC1: move `FMU_CH1_PROT` off B.Cu where it crosses the MVDD pour (an inner layer, or around the pour), so the bottom pour is one piece again.
2. ESC2: move the B.Cu `FD6288_VS1` track that separates Q15's copper from Q17/Q19's.
3. On every ESC, add a block of at least 10 vias (0.3 mm drill) from the shunt-side top copper straight into the bottom copper under the FETs, and route the new `+5.2V`/PWM/gate tracks on In2/In3 where they cross the MVDD pour, so the top pour reaches the FET rows again.
4. Re-check with the island pictures: one colour per layer per ESC.

## Major

### M1. VBATT distribution is thin everywhere, not only at the neck (carried over from v0.10)

**Where:** F.Cu rear pours `VBATT_REAR_TOP_L/R` (y 125.3–135) and the rear strip at y 135–140.

Between the XT90 + pin and the side strips, VBATT runs on the top layer only. In2/In3 carry VBATT only in the side strips and in a small rear-centre band (x 129–162, y 135–140).
The rear band is about 9.7 mm tall before the cut-outs for J6, J7, J16 and the gate tracks.

Solver, 20 A per ESC, 1 oz copper:
- Right side: 200 mV lost by the rear-right corner (5 mΩ, about 8 W at 40 A).
- Left side: 300 mV lost across the rear-left pour before the neck, plus 40–65 mV in the 4.5 mm strip behind C112.
- For comparison the GND return, which has four layers, drops 3–15 mV and dissipates 0.9 W in total at 80 A.

At hover currents (5 A per ESC) this is fine: about 50 mV and 0.5 W per side.
At the 4 × 20 A the ESCs are specified for, the VBATT copper alone dissipates 20–27 W even with B1 fixed.

**Fix:** In1 and In4 are already full GND planes, so give In2 and In3 to VBATT across the whole rear band (y 125–140) and join them to the XT90 + pin, which is a through-hole on every layer.
That triples the copper in the bottleneck. Add via rows where the top pour, the inner planes and the side strips overlap.
Also state the copper weight on the PCBWay order (1 oz inner at least; the stackup in the file says 35 µm on all six layers, but the order form decides).
Going to 2 oz outer copper would conflict with the 0.127–0.15 mm tracks and 0.15 mm clearances, so fix this in layout rather than in copper weight.

### M2. INA180 current-sense amplifiers sit at their absolute maximum on a 6S pack (carried over)

**Where:** U4, U10, U14, U18 (INA180B1IDBVR), inputs on VBATT and MVDD.

INA180 datasheet (SBOS741H) section 7.1: analog inputs, common-mode, absolute maximum 26 V. Section 7.3: recommended maximum 26 V.
A full 6S pack is 25.2 V, which leaves 0.8 V. Any ringing or regenerative rise at the ESC input goes straight past the absolute maximum.
The inputs are wired directly, with no series resistance or clamp.

**Fix:** either follow section 9.3.1 of the datasheet ("Common-Mode Transients Greater Than 26 V": about 10 Ω in series with each input plus Zener clamps), or change to a current-sense amplifier rated for at least 40 V common-mode.
A replacement is unlikely to be pin-compatible with INA180 pinout B (1 IN+, 2 GND, 3 IN−, 4 OUT, 5 VS), so check the footprint.

### M3. Both buck converters have an RT resistor outside the datasheet range (carried over)

**Where:** R43 (U5, 10 V buck) and R46 (U8, 5.2 V buck), both 200 kΩ.

LMR51450 datasheet (SLUSEP7) table 8-1 gives 84.5 kΩ for 200 kHz, 31.6 kΩ for 495 kHz, and "open or pull-up to > 1.0 V" for 500 kHz.
The allowed range is 200 kHz to 1.1 MHz. Equation 2 puts 200 kΩ at about 94 kHz, below the minimum.
The inductors (L1 10 µH, L2 4.7 µH) and output capacitors match the datasheet's 500 kHz table, so 500 kHz was clearly the intent.
What the part does with 200 kΩ is not specified: it may treat the pin as open (500 kHz) or run near 94 kHz.
At 94 kHz the ripple current would be about 6.4 A peak-to-peak in L1 and 5.6 A in L2, which works on the bench at light load and fails under load.

**Fix:** measure the SW-node frequency on a v0.10 board (L1 or L2 pad). Then either leave R43/R46 unfitted (RT open = 500 kHz) or fit 31.6 kΩ.

### M4. 26 new vias inside small solder pads, six of them on QFN pins (new in v0.11)

**Where** (via centre inside the pad; all same-net, so DRC is silent):
- FD6288 pins, pad 0.25 mm wide with a 0.40–0.45 mm via: U12.19 (173.96, 114.30), U12.9 (177.64, 112.70), U16.1 (114.31, 110.50), U3.1 (173.70, 78.50), U3.13 (177.72, 75.80), U3.15 (177.50, 76.90).
- 0603 pads: C42.2, C43.1, C51.2, C82.2, C83.1, C117.1, R33.1, R91.1, R108.1, R108.2.
- JST-GH signal pads: J15.2, J16.2, J16.3. Also J16.4, J16.5 (mounting tabs).
- Others: Q17.1, Q25.3, U18.1, F1.1, C30.2 (three vias).

v0.10 had 10 such vias, nine of them on large GND or tab pads. The tenth, on FD6288 pin U2.19 at (114.30, 74.70), is still there and makes seven QFN pins in all.
The new ones come from the scripted re-routing.
Open vias in a pad pull solder away from the joint. On a QFN pin the via is wider than the pad, so there is no mask dam to the neighbouring pins either.

**Fix:** move each via at least 0.3 mm off its pad with a short track (preferred), or order filled and capped via-in-pad from PCBWay, which costs extra and applies to the whole board.

### M5. Fan output is unfused battery voltage on thin tracks (new in v0.11, from moving the fan to VBATT)

**Where:** J14 pin 1 (132.6, 120.0). Feed: In3.Cu 0.4 mm track from (146.2, 128.4) via (146.2, 118.5) and (136.0, 118.5), then a 0.3 mm stub.

A pinched fan lead puts a dead short across the pack through a 0.3–0.4 mm track, about 25 mm of it on an inner layer. The track is the fuse, inside the board.
Q1 is an AO3400A rated 30 V drain-source (AOS datasheet), which is only 4.8 V above a full pack. D15 clamps the drain to VBATT, so the FET sees whatever the bus does.

**Fix:** add a small fuse or PTC rated for 30 V or more (a 0.5 A 1206 part) in series with J14 pin 1, and change Q1 to a 40–60 V SOT-23 MOSFET with the same pinout and a logic-level gate.

### M6. VBATT copper 0.2 mm from the board edge and under the rear mounting screws (edge: carried over; holes and edge vias: new)

**Where:** F.Cu and In2/In3 VBATT fills along the left, right and rear edges; VBATT vias at (190.75, 120.75), (190.75, 123.0), (190.75, 126.0); rear mounting holes at (102.61, 137.5) and (188.75, 137.5).

- The VBATT zone outlines extend past the board outline, so the fill is clipped at the 0.2 mm rule on three edges. v0.10 was the same; the three edge vias are new.
  0.2 mm is at the limit of what a routed edge guarantees; any burr or a conductive frame edge then touches battery positive.
- On the top side the VBATT pour comes within 0.3 mm of the wall of both rear 3.0 mm holes. On the bottom side the same holes are ringed by GND.
  A metal screw and standoff through one of those holes has only solder mask between it and a direct battery short.
- Phase-node vias sit 1.3–1.5 mm from the walls of all four corner holes, which is under an M3 screw head (2.75 mm radius).

**Fix:** pull VBATT and phase copper back at least 0.5 mm from the outline on all layers, and add a keep-out of about 3.5 mm radius for everything except GND around the four corner holes on both outer layers. Use nylon hardware until then.

## Nit

1. "USBC" silkscreen beside J21, which is a JST-GH port. "USB" would be clearer.
2. ESC DBG labels count clockwise (1 FR, 2 BR, 3 BL, 4 FL). ArduPilot's quad-X motor order is 1 FR, 2 BL, 3 FL, 4 BR.
3. 304 reference designators are 0.8 mm tall with a 0.10 mm stroke, and "GND" beside D18 is 0.5 mm. PCBWay's usual minimum stroke is 0.15 mm. DRC also reports 182 silk overlaps and 199 silk-over-copper clips.
4. D18 only lights if something drives J21 pin 6. On CubePilot's carrier that pin is FMU_LED_AMBER, an output. Here J1 pin 2 is not connected, so D18 does nothing.
5. BOM field leftovers: C31/C32 "Voltage = 1kV", C113 "Voltage = 5%", D1 manufacturer "hongjiacheng" against "Taiwan Semiconductor". Four new parts have no LCSC number: U22, C116, R131, R134.
6. The bootstrap diodes are 1N5819WS Schottkys. Reverse leakage rises steeply with temperature and drains the bootstrap capacitor at 100 % duty. A low-leakage fast diode is the usual choice. Unchanged from v0.10 and from the VimDrones design.
7. Thermal vias in the U5/U8 pad and the vias in U21's power pads are 0.3 mm pads on 0.2 mm drills (0.05 mm ring). They match TI's land patterns and were built in v0.10.
8. The fan connector carries pack voltage. Its silkscreen says "V", the same as the 5 V ports. Consider "VBAT".
9. `review/renders/` is untracked, and the project files still carry "v0.10" in their names (explained in the README).
10. The project DRC rules should be tightened to PCBWay's limits so that DRC catches items like m8 and M6 next time.

## Checked and OK

**Polarity and pin 1**
- **XT90 (J18):** correct. Facing the mating end with the board top up, the VBATT pad (150.45, 122.1) is on the right.
  The XT90PW-M has its flat (+) side on the left when its mounting face is down, so mounted upside-down on the bottom its + lands on VBATT.
  Sources: AMASS catalogue p. 24 (drawing with the "−" mark, and photo) and PCBWay's v0.10 photo 7, which shows the same part top-mounted with the chamfered (−) end on the right. That photo also explains the v0.10 reversal.
  Still worth holding a real connector against a 1:1 print before ordering, given the history.
- **J1 pin numbering:** every connected pin matches CubePilot's reference netlist (39 CAN2_L, 41 CAN2_H, 77 CAN1_L, 79 CAN1_H, 29 VBUS, 31 D+, 33 D−, 55/59 valid flags, 61/63 5 V in, 24 alarm, 66 IO-CH8, 62 PPM, 47 battery voltage, serial 1 and 2, FMU CH1–5, GND 13/15).
  Pad pattern matches the reference package (0.25 × 1.15 pads, 0.5 pitch, rows 5.15 apart, tabs at ±12, bosses on the odd side). Pin 1 is at the front, odd row toward the Cube centre, even row on the Cube's right: same as the reference with the Cube arrow pointing forward.
  J1's pads and nets are identical to v0.10, which ran a Cube.
- **J1 part:** DF17(1.0H)-80DP-0.5V(57) is what CubePilot's reference uses.
- D1 (cathode to VBATT), C112 (+ to VBATT), D15/D16 flyback diodes, the 12 bootstrap diodes (anode to +10 V), LEDs D3/D17/D18, all 24 MOSFETs (source/gate/drain against high-side and low-side position).
- "V" and "G" silkscreen marks on J8, J9, J12, J13, J14, J28, J30, J31, J32 are at the correct ends. CH1–CH5 labels match FMU_CH1–5. "Pin 1" label on J1 is at pin 1.

**IC pinouts against datasheets (netlist and footprint)**
- U21 TPS2121RUX: pins and land pattern match SLVSEA3F fig. 6-2 and the RUX0012A drawing, including orientation on the bottom side. ST and OV1/OV2 to GND is the documented way to leave them unused.
- U21 priority: CP2 = 1.57 V is above VREF (1.06 V), so the part compares PR1 with CP2 (table 9-3). PR1 = 0.27 × VBUS stays below CP2 until VBUS exceeds 5.8 V, so the battery rail wins. USB takes over when +5.2 V falls to about 4.5 V. ILM 56.2 kΩ gives 2.0 A typical (equation 2). SS 100 nF present.
- U5/U8 LMR51450SDRR: pins, land pattern and orientation match. FB dividers give 10.0 V and 5.21 V from the 0.8 V reference. Feed-forward 33 pF + 1 kΩ matches table 9-2. Boot capacitors 100 nF 50 V. Output capacitance after DC-bias derating meets table 9-2. U8 EN to VIN through 100 kΩ is allowed. PG open is allowed.
- U22 TCM809J: SOT-23 pins 1 GND, 2 RESET, 3 VDD; 4.00 V threshold; push-pull output driving Q27. Active-low VBUS_VALID to J1 pin 59.
- U1/U9/U13/U17 STM32L431KC: all 32 pins match DS11453 fig. 14. BOOT0 (PH3) pulled down with 10 kΩ, NRST has 100 nF, clock enters on PA0/CK_IN from a 24 MHz oscillator (no load capacitors needed), SWD on PA13/PA14, CAN on PA11/PA12. PWM pins form correct TIM1 complementary pairs (PA10/PB1, PA9/PB0, PA8/PA7).
- U2/U3/U12/U16: netlist matches the Fortior FD6288Q pin table (V1.6, table 1-2). VCC 10 V is inside 5–20 V. Logic-high threshold 2.7 V works from 3.3 V.
- U7/U11/U15/U19 TJA1051TK/3: pins match; VCC 5.2 V is inside 4.5–5.5 V; VIO 3.3 V; S pulled low.
- U4/U10/U14/U18 INA180B1: pinout B matches (but see M2).
- U6 AP2112K-3.3, U20 USBLC6-2SC6 (pin 5 deliberately open), Q1/Q2/Q27/Q28 SOT-23 pinouts.
- Decoupling distances: every MCU supply pin, FD6288 VCC, CAN transceiver, oscillator and INA180 has a capacitor within 2.6 mm. Bootstrap capacitors are 1.5–3.5 mm from their pins.

**Interfaces**
- CAN2: J1 39/41 to CANL/CANH of all four transceivers with the right polarity. CAN1: J10/J11 pins 1–4 = 5 V, H, L, GND.
- Serial 1/2: Cube TX on connector pin 2, RX on pin 3, CTS 4, RTS 5.
- USB: D+ and D− straight through U20 without a swap. VBUS to J1 pin 29.
- VBUS_VALID and BRICK_VALID are active-low, matching CubePilot's `!VBUS_OK` and `!5V_A_OK`.
- I2C is unused, so there are no pull-ups to check.
- Buzzer and fan drivers: gate series and pull-down resistors, flyback diodes, buzzer discharge resistor present.

**Power and gate drive**
- GND return: 3–15 mV rise from the low-side FET sources to the XT90 at 20 A per ESC. Peak density 5 A/mm. The stitching removed near J6 and the back-right pour has not opened the return path.
- Phase node to motor pad, all phases solved on ESC1, ESC2 and ESC4: 21–27 mV from the high-side source and 10–14 mV from the low-side drain at 20 A, through 12–19 vias per phase. (Shunt to FET drains is B2.)
- Gate resistors are 1.6–2.3 mm from their gates on all 24 FETs. HO/LO runs are 14–34 mm, mostly on In2/In3 between the ground planes.
- ESC3 `FD6288_VS1` dog-leg past J7 and ESC2 `FD6288_LO1` past J6: continuous, clear of the connector pads, widths 0.3 and 0.2 mm.
- ESC2 `FD6288_VS2` is the longest gate-return net (108 mm of track in total, 19 mm of it at 0.127 mm). The thin sections carry only the BEMF divider and the driver's VS pin. Resistance and inductance are small beside the 10 Ω gate resistors.
- 0.127 mm tracks: 17 segments, on three nets (ESC1 VS2, ESC2 VS2, ESC2 LO3). Within PCBWay's standard capability for 1 oz.
- Fuses F1/F2 (5 A) are ample for the buck input currents.

**Mechanical**
- Outline 91.14 × 90.00 mm. Corner holes 3.0 mm on 86.14 × 85.00 mm. Cube holes 1.8 mm on 30 × 30 mm, centred on (145, 95).
- Only J1 is on the top side inside the Cube outline (CubePilot's keep-out is ±19.5 mm).
- No courtyard overlaps. MOSFET courtyards are more than 1.5 mm apart at the 9.4 mm pitch.
- Both sides carry parts (104 top, 252 bottom), as in v0.10, so two reflow passes are unavoidable. The XT90 is the only through-hole part.

## Not verified

- **JSM6288Q** (the part in the BOM): LCSC serves only a stub page. Compatibility with FD6288Q rests on v0.10 having run with it.
- **TPHR8504PL** datasheet (Toshiba link dead, Mouser timed out). Pinout taken as the standard SOP Advance arrangement and from v0.10.
- **ASAAIG oscillator** primary datasheet (Abracon links dead). Pin functions come from a distributor copy found by search: pin 1 enable (open = on), 2 GND, 3 output, 4 VDD.
- **Hirose DF17** catalogue (403). Land pattern and numbering were checked against CubePilot's Eagle package instead.
- **JST GH and SH** catalogue PDFs are encrypted. Pin 1 positions rest on the unchanged footprints and the working USB port on v0.10.
- **Littelfuse 0451005.MRL** interrupt rating at 25 V DC, **C112** ripple-current rating, **L1/L2** saturation current against the buck's 9.6 A maximum current limit.
- **XT90PW-M hole pattern** (pin-to-peg distance, hole sizes): only the catalogue outline was available, not the detailed specification.
- **Inside the Cube:** pull-ups on the valid and over-current pins, CAN termination.
- **PCBWay limits** quoted here (0.2 mm copper-to-edge, 0.25 mm hole spacing, 0.15 mm silkscreen stroke) are the commonly published ones, not re-checked today. Confirm against their current capability page.
- **Thermal behaviour**, switching waveforms and AM32 firmware pin mapping. The copper model is DC only.
- **KiCad was still running** (project manager open, PCB editor closed, no PCB lock file) when the silkscreen commit was made. If anything was saved afterwards, `git status` will show it.
