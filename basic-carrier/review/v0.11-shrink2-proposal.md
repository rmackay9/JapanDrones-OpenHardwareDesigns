# v0.11 second shrink: tighter ESCs (analysis only)

Starting point: commit 8f20da8, **94 x 90 mm** board, with the XT90 overhanging 5 mm at the back. Coordinates are KiCad page mm
(front edge y 50, back edge y 140). No layout has been edited for this proposal.

## Measured: the four ESC power stages

All four ESCs are the same layout: the front ones are mirrored front-to-back, and the right ones are mirrored left-to-right.
They are slightly looser on the right (pitch 10.2-10.5 mm vs 9.9-10.0 mm on the left).

| MOSFETs | 6x TPHR8504PL (SOP-Advance 5x5), all on the **bottom**. One high-side row (courtyard 7.5 w x 5.6 d) and one low-side row rotated 90 deg (5.6 w x 7.5 d) |
| MOSFET pitch (one phase per column) | 9.9-10.0 mm (left ESCs), 10.2-10.5 mm (right ESCs) |
| Gaps, high-side row | 2.4-3.0 mm between courtyards. The gate resistor + pulldown pairs sit in these gaps |
| Gaps, low-side row | 4.3-4.6 mm between courtyards (partly empty) |
| High-side row to low-side row | 1.8 mm |
| Motor pads (J2/J19/J22/J25, top) | 3 pads 4.0 x 2.5 mm, **10 mm pitch**, 2.5 mm from the edge. Their courtyard is 1.1 mm in front of the high-side row, which is on the other side of the board |
| Bulk caps | 8x 10 uF 1206 on top, in a 2x4 block at the outer edge (9.5 x 9.5 mm), behind the MOSFETs |

Battery reaches the ESCs through the U-shaped VBATT strip (top/In2/In3) along the sides and back. In1/In4 are solid GND. There are via fields under the motor pads and the MOSFET tabs |

One ESC is **~31 mm wide** (x 100-131) and **~41 mm deep** (motor pads to the ESC_DBG connector). Front and back
ESCs plus the 8 mm LED-connector band between them make up the full 90 mm depth on each side.

Free-space check (`tools/layout_slack.py`): this scans horizontal and vertical lines across the board, per side, and adds up the space
not covered by part courtyards, leaving 0.4 mm gaps. That is an **upper bound**, because it ignores tracks, pours and vias:
- **Width:** at least 12.7 mm free on every horizontal line. The tightest line is the back MOSFET row, which also crosses the XT90 body.
- **Depth:** at least 19.9 mm free on every vertical line. The tightest line is x ~129: MOSFETs, CAN chips, the ESC MCUs and the MOSFETs again.

**The centre column also limits the depth:**
- Front: buck/USB/LDO/CAN parts take 29 mm from the front edge to the front Cube holes.
- Middle: the Cube holes take 30 mm.
- Back: D1, C112 and the XT90 pads take 30 mm.

So shrinking only the ESCs makes the board narrower, but **not shorter** unless the centre column shrinks as well.

## Options

### A. Tighter MOSFET rows → narrower board (recommended first step)
- Phase pitch 10 → **8.5 mm**:
  - High-side gaps go from 2.4 to ~0.9 mm. The gate resistor/pulldown pairs move into the low-side row gaps (4.3 mm, mostly empty) or behind the row.
  - Motor pad pitch also goes 10 → 8.5 mm. That leaves 4.5 mm between pads, still fine for soldering motor wires.
- The rows behind (cap block, FD6288, MCU/shunt/INA) also come ~3 mm in per side. The free-space check says there is room.
- Also tighten the high-side to low-side row gap (1.8 → ~0.8 mm) while re-placing.
- **Result: ~88 x 90 mm** (-6 mm wide).
- Effort ~1.5-2 days: re-place and re-route all four power stages (mostly copy one ESC, then mirror). Risk low-medium.

### B. A + close the LED band + XT90 forward under the Cube → ~88 x 82-84 mm
- Move the five LED JST-GH connectors (J4/J6/J7/J15/J16) out of the 8 mm band (for example, to the outer edges beside the cap blocks, or onto the bottom).
  The front and back ESCs then move together. Side columns: ~82 mm.
- The centre has to shrink by the same amount:
  - Move the XT90 forward by ~6-8 mm. Its body (bottom side) can go under the Cube area, which only has low parts on the bottom there.
  - Its pins come up through the board just behind the Cube's rear edge.
  - D1 and C112 have to move sideways or be split into smaller caps near each ESC. Each ESC already has 8x 10 uF.
- The front centre (buck, USB, Remote ID, buzzer connectors) stays.
- Effort ~3 days. Risk medium: the power path around the XT90 is redone again, 26 tracks cross the band, and C112's replacement needs a decision.

### C. B + a deeper ESC rework → ~88 x 76-78 mm (only if size really matters)
- Put the 8x 10 uF bulk caps on **top, directly over the low-side MOSFETs** (one row of 8 vertical 1206s, ~4.6 mm deep).
  This frees the 9.5 mm cap block area and also shortens the commutation loop, which is good electrically.
- Slide the high-side row partly under the motor pads (opposite sides of the board), keeping the phase via fields.
- Rotate the 2512 shunt. Replace the 9.4 mm ESC_DBG JST-SH connectors with SWD test pads or Tag-Connect, or drop them.
- Pull the centre front together too (move the USB, Remote ID and buzzer connectors into the space freed in the ESCs).
- Effort 5+ days, a near-complete re-layout of all four ESCs. Risk medium-high: the thermal and current paths change, and motor wires
  would be soldered directly above the MOSFETs (the iron's heat can disturb the MOSFET joints underneath; a fixture helps).

## Costs and limits of tightening
- **Heat:** at hover each MOSFET dissipates roughly 0.1-0.3 W (Rds(on) ~0.85 mOhm, plus switching losses), so there is little heat to
  spread. Going from 10 mm to 8.5 mm pitch leaves each phase ~15% less copper, which I don't expect to matter. Keep the thermal vias under
  the drain tabs and the bottom pours.
- **Current:** phase pours shrink from ~9 to ~7.5 mm wide on two outer layers. That is still OK for motor peaks of tens of amps.
  The VBATT feed is the real limit, and A/B do not narrow it (it was re-widened on In2/In3 in rev 1).
- **Assembly:** JLC/PCBWay need ~0.3-0.5 mm between parts. 0.8-1.0 mm between MOSFET courtyards is fine for reflow. Hot-air rework
  of one MOSFET will heat its neighbours more.
- **Mounting holes:** these move with the outline. With motor pads at 8.5 mm pitch, the corner holes stay clear.

## Recommendation
Do **A** next: a solid -6 mm in width at low risk, with the ESC layout and power path essentially unchanged. After that, decide on **B**
by how much shorter the frame needs to be. B is where the extra depth comes from, and the real decision there is the XT90/C112 area.
Skip **C** unless the size targets demand it.
