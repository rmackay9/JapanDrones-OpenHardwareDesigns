# BasicCarrier v0.11 — connection review

Date: 2026-11-26. Design state: commit 183ac4b (v0.11 + clean-ups).

This review was prompted by the v0.10 mistake where the Cube's CAN2 H/L pins
were wired to the ESC transceivers' CANL/CANH. ERC and DRC cannot catch that
kind of error, because every net is "connected". So this review checks what
each pin *is* against what it is wired to.

## How it was done

1. Netlist exported with `kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml`.
2. `tools/check_netlist.py` (new, standard-library Python) checks every net for:
   - **CAN polarity**: the net label, IC pin functions (CANH/CANL) and Cube
     pin names (from the pin-mapping sheet) must all agree on H vs L.
   - **TX/RX direction**: TX→TX or RX→RX between drivers, and transceiver
     TXD fed from an RX pin. MCU pins are resolved to their alternate
     functions (STM32L431: PA12=CAN1_TX, PA11=CAN1_RX, PB6=USART1_TX…).
   - **Power/ground pins** on the wrong kind of net, and power inputs left floating.
   - **Outputs tied directly to GND/rails**, and several outputs driving one net.
   - **Single-pin nets** and floating inputs.
3. It also generates a pin table for every connector, showing what's on the far
   end of each pin, and compares J1 pin by pin against
   `../cubeorange-df17-pinmapping.ods`.
4. U21 (TPS2121 power mux) was checked against TI datasheet SLVSEA3F (Aug 2020).

Run it after any schematic change:

    python tools/check_netlist.py --out review/v0.11-netlist-report.md

The exit code is non-zero if any ERROR is found. The full generated output is
in [v0.11-netlist-report.md](v0.11-netlist-report.md).

**Regression check:** run against the `v0.10` tag, the script reports
`can-polarity` errors on both CAN_H_2 and CAN_L_2. It would have caught the
original bug. Against v0.11 it reports 0 errors.

## Items for Randy

### A. U21 ST pin tied to GND — ERC error is a false positive

Datasheet pin table: *"ST … Status output indicating which channel is
selected. Connect to GND if not required."* So it is an open-drain output,
and grounding it is the documented way to leave it unused. The ERC error
("Output and Power output are connected") appeared once GND got a PWR_FLAG.
It is caused by the symbol calling ST a push-pull Output. **Done (2026-11-26):** the ST pin
in this project's embedded copy of the TPS2121 symbol is now "Passive". The
global library is untouched. KiCad's default pin matrix also flags
open-collector vs power-output, so "Passive" was needed. ERC: 0 errors.

### B. U21 current limit is set to ~1.5 A (1.0–2.0 A)

R134 = 80.6 kΩ on ILIM. Datasheet table (TPS2121): R_ILM = 80 kΩ gives
ILM min 1 A / typ 1.5 A / max 2 A. Everything on the POW_SEL rail shares this
limit: the Cube's VDD_5V_IN (J1 61/63), both CAN GPS ports (J10/J11) and the
RC receiver (J3). A Cube Orange plus two GPS units plus a receiver is
typically under 1 A. But the worst-case 1 A minimum leaves little margin
for start-up inrush or extra CAN peripherals. **Consider** 44.2 kΩ
(2–3 A) or 29.8 kΩ (3–4 A) if anything else will hang off those ports.
SS: C110 = 100 nF gives ~0.8 V/ms output slew (datasheet table 9-1), which is
fine.

**Done (2026-11-26):** R134 changed to 56.2 kΩ (UNI-ROYAL 0603WAF5622T5E, 1%
0603), giving ILM ≈1.5–2.5 A (typ 2.0 A, from I = 65.2/R^0.861). This doubles
the guaranteed headroom over 80.6k while keeping fault current closer to what
the 0.5 mm POW_SEL traces can carry (the 25 mm inner-layer run is the weak
spot). 44.2k (2.0–3.0 A) was considered too. The LCSC field is blank until a stock
code is confirmed. Note: a future LTE modem + Raspberry Pi daughter board
(1–2.5 A plus ~2 A transmit peaks) should get its own 5 V regulator from VBATT
rather than share this rail. The +5.2V rail itself comes from U8 (LMR51450,
5 A buck from the 10 V rail made by U5, also an LMR51450).

(An earlier session note said ILIM/SS were unconnected. That was wrong: a
konnect query mis-traced those pins. The netlist shows R134 and C110 in place.)

### C. Power-mux priority — confirmed sensible

PR1 = VBUS × 10k/37k ≈ 1.35 V, and CP2 = +5.2V × 10k/25k ≈ 2.08 V. With
both present PR1 < CP2, so IN2 (+5.2V from the battery buck) powers the Cube.
USB only powers it when the battery is absent. That matches the intent of
"battery first, USB for bench use".

### D. Cube "valid" signals (J1 55, 59)

- J1 55 VDD_BRICK_VALID ← VBATT_VALID: Q28 (2N7002) pulls it low when
  +5.2V is present (gate divider R137 100k / R138 1M).
- J1 59 VBUS_VALID ← Q27 pulls it low when USB VBUS is present.

These are correct if the Cube treats both as active-low ("low = valid"),
which is how the Pixhawk-standard carrier interface defines them. **Worth
confirming** against the Cube Orange carrier-board docs, since a wrong
polarity here would make the Cube ignore its main supply.

### E. No main-battery current sense to the Cube

J1 49 BATT_CURRENT_SENS_PROT is unconnected, and the sheet doesn't assign it.
Battery voltage (J1 47) is wired through R123/R124. The four INA180s measure
ESC phase currents locally, so ArduPilot will have voltage but no current or
consumed-mAh. **Confirm** this is intended. If not, it's a candidate for v0.12.

**Randy (2026-11-26):** probably left off to save space. Deferred, no change for now.

### F. Duplicate net names (cosmetic)

ERC "multiple net names" warnings. On the Connectors sheet, local labels
`FAN_1` (fan block → J14 pin 1, at ~54.6,147.3 and 25.4,180.3 mm) share a
wire with the VBATT power symbol. `BUZZ_1` (buzzer block → J9 pin 1, at
~97.8,147.3 and 66.0,180.3 mm) shares one with +5.2V. Likewise
`BUCK_PATH_VIN` is on VBATT and `VBUS`/`VBUS_PWR` are joined. In each case
KiCad keeps the power name, so the PCB has no FAN_1/BUZZ_1 nets.
Connectivity is correct; the labels just give one net two names.

**Done (2026-11-26):** the four FAN_1/BUZZ_1 labels were replaced with VBATT
and +5.2V power symbols, so J14 pin 1 and J9 pin 1 now show their real rail.
A label hiding the rail name is partly how the fan ended up on 5.2V in v0.10.
Nets are unchanged. Also done: the two BUCK_PATH_VIN labels
(Power sheet) are now VBATT power symbols, and the hierarchical label/sheet
pins VBUS_PWR are renamed VBUS to match the USB sheet's VBUS labels. The PCB
net name is still /USBSheet/VBUS, so no PCB update is needed. ERC now has no
"multiple net names" warnings.

### G. USB connector J21 pinout

J21 is a 6-pin JST-GH: 1 = VBUS (via F3), 2 = D+, 3 = D−, 4 = GND, 5 = NC,
6 = D18 anode. The pin-mapping sheet says "USB … JST-GH 4-pin". The D+/D−
order through U20 (USBLC6) is consistent with J1 31/33. **Check** that the
USB cable you'll use matches this 6-pin order.

**Randy (2026-11-26):** the 6-pin layout is intentional. It matches the
standard Cube carrier-board USB connector.

## What the checker can't do (yet)

- It knows pin functions only from symbol pin names, a small table of MCU
  alternate functions, and the Cube pin-mapping sheet. External connectors
  with numbered pins (JST etc.) are checked by the human-readable tables, not
  automatically. Adding expected pinouts per connector type (e.g. "GH-4 CAN =
  5V, H, L, GND") would make those checks automatic too.
- It doesn't check component values (dividers, current limits). Items B/C
  above were checked by hand against the datasheet.
- Symbol pin types come from the libraries. Wrong pin types (like U21 ST)
  give false positives.

## Bench findings on v0.10 hardware (2026-11-26) and fixes

Randy's tests on the built v0.10 board:

1. **VBUS_nVALID always "USB present"** (works on the standard carrier).
   With the battery on and USB unplugged, the USB VBUS pin (J21-1) measured
   **1.9 V**. Q27 (2N7002, Vgs(th) 1–2.5 V) was driven from VBUS through
   R135 100k / R136 1M, so it switched on and held J1-59 low.
   Likely back-feed: the Cube's D+ pull-up through U20 (USBLC6) steering
   diodes into its VBUS pin. Not yet isolated; Cube-out / lift-U20-pin-5 tests
   are pending. CubePilot's reference carrier has no ESD part on D+/D−, feeds
   the Cube's VBUS pin from its power-selection module rather than the raw
   connector, and takes !VBUS_OK from a proper detector.
2. **Cube reboots when USB or battery is connected or disconnected.** Causes
   (design review): no capacitance on POW_SEL; U21 only changed over from
   +5.2V to USB once +5.2V had sagged to ~3.4 V (PR1 = VBUS·10/37 vs
   CP2 = 5V2·10/25).

Changes made for v0.11 (USBSheet):

- **U20 pin 5 (VBUS) disconnected** (no-connect). Data lines are still
  clamped through the device's internal rail-to-rail diodes and transil,
  at a slightly higher voltage. This removes the suspected back-feed path.
- **USB-present detector:** R135/R136 removed. U22 (TCM809JENB713, 4.00 V,
  push-pull active-low reset, SOT-23; LM809-compatible pinout) is powered from
  VBUS and drives Q27's gate. When VBUS > ~4.0 V, the output goes high, Q27
  turns on and J1-59 is pulled low (USB valid). At the 1.9 V back-feed level
  the output stays low and Q27 stays off. The open-drain polarity to the Cube
  is unchanged. LCSC code not confirmed (field blank).
- **C116 47 µF 1206 X5R 25 V (GRM31CR61E476ME44L, as C38) + C117 100 nF**
  on POW_SEL at U21's output.
- **R131 15k → 23.2k** (0603WAF2322T5E, LCSC blank). CP2 = 5V2·10/33.2 =
  1.57 V > PR1 = 1.35 V, so the battery is still preferred. Changeover to USB
  now happens when +5.2V falls to ~4.49 V (with VBUS = 5.0 V).
- A PWR_FLAG on VBUS (the USB connector drives it) keeps ERC clean.

Unchanged by request: USB-only operation still powers the Cube, both GPS ports
(J10/J11) and RC (J3) through U21 IN1 → POW_SEL.

Rework to try on the v0.10 board: lift U20 pin 5, tack 22–47 µF across
POW_SEL/GND (e.g. J10 pins 1–4), and change R131 to ~22–24k.

Bench comparison (Randy, 2026-11-26): the standard carrier's USB VBUS reads ~0 V with
USB unplugged and ~5 V when plugged in. The BasicCarrier reads 1.9 V (battery only)
and 4.97 V (USB). The supervisor threshold was lowered from 4.38 V (M) to
4.00 V (J variant). That leaves margin for USB sag through F3 and the cable
under load while staying well above the 1.9 V back-feed.
