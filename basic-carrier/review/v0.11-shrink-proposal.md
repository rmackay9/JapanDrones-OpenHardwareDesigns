# v0.11 board shrink proposal

Analysis only; no layout changes yet. Board coordinates are KiCad page coordinates in mm
(x to the right, y downward). **Front = y 50 edge (J2/J19 motor pads), back = y 140 edge (XT90 J18).**

## Where we are now

| | value |
|---|---|
| Board outline | 100 x 90 mm (x 96..196, y 50..140) |
| XT90 J18 body | bottom side, courtyard y 126.2..152.1, so it sticks **12 mm** past the back edge |
| Front edge to XT90 mating face | **102 mm**. This is what sets the frame length |
| Corner holes | 3.0 mm at (99,53) (193,53) (99,137) (193,137), so a 94 x 84 pattern |
| Cube holes | 1.8 mm at (130/160, 80/110), a 30 x 30 pattern centred on (145,95). **Kept as is** |

Layout in one line: four ESCs in the corners (motor pads on the front/back edges, two FET rows, then
cap columns, FD6288, MCU and shunt towards the middle). The Cube/J1 and power parts are in a centre
column about 28 mm wide (x 131..160). There is an 8 mm band at y 91..99 between the front and back
ESCs. It holds the five LED connectors and the "space for SD card and usb connector" note.

## What sets each edge

| Edge | What sets it | Can move in without moving parts |
|---|---|---|
| **Front** (y 50) | J2/J19 motor pads (copper from y 50.5); FD6288 phase pours drawn to the edge; L2 at 50.85; R142 at 50.65; holes at y 53 | **0 mm**. The ESC power stages and the 5.2 V buck already touch it |
| **Back** (y 140) | J22/J25 motor pads (to 139.6); phase pours to the edge; XT90 locating slots at y 138; XT90 body overhangs to 152 | **0 mm** for the board. The XT90 can come **12 mm** in (see A) |
| **Left** (x 96) | Corner holes (x 99); 10 uF cap columns at x 100.1; J16 at 100.09; RSHUNT5 at 100.24 | **~3 mm** if the holes move (see W). Only GND/VBATT stitching vias are there (35), no tracks |
| **Right** (x 196) | Corner holes (x 193); J4 to 189.9; cap columns to 189.83; U14 189.85; RSHUNT3 189.63 | **~3 mm** if the holes move (see W). Only stitching vias (43), no tracks |

The depth is really set by two stacks that both use the full 90 mm:
- **Side columns:** front ESC block y 50..91 (41 mm), the 8 mm mid band, back ESC block y 99..140 (41 mm).
  These can't go below about 83 mm without redesigning an ESC.
- **Centre column:** 5.2 V buck/USB/LDO/CAN parts y 50.6..80 (29 mm) + Cube holes 80..110 (30 mm) +
  C112/D1/F1 (107.5..124) + XT90 pads/slots (126.6..138).

## Proposal

### W: trim the width, 100 to 94 mm (low effort, ~1-2 h, low risk)
- New outline x 99..193 (94 mm). Left cap columns keep 1.1 mm to the edge and the right side keeps ~3 mm.
- Move the corner holes to (101.5, 52.5) (190.5, 52.5) (101.5, 137.5) (190.5, 137.5), an **89 x 85 pattern**,
  2.5 mm from each edge. With an M3 pan head (r ~3 mm) that leaves about 1 mm to the J2/J19/J22/J25 motor
  pad copper and the Q4/Q10/Q16/Q22 courtyards. Insulating washers are a good idea because the
  motor phase pads are close. M2.5 would give more margin.
- Work: redraw Edge.Cuts and the hole circles, pull the board-edge zones back, delete or move the stitching vias
  outside the new edge, refill, DRC. No routing changes.

### A: bring the XT90 fully onto the board, mating face flush with the back edge (recommended, ~4-8 h, medium-low risk)
Board stays 90 deep, but **the frame length goes from 102 to 90 mm (-12 mm)**. The ESCs are not touched.
- Move J18 forward 12 mm, to (145, 126). Pads go to y 117.1, the slots to y 126 and the body to y 114.2..140.
- Bottom side under the body (x 134.4..155.7, y 114..140) must be clear:
  - **C112** (330 uF, 10x10.5 can) moves to the **top side** at about (145, 126): y 120.5..131.5, between the XT90
    pads and slots. It is about 6 mm behind the Cube body (assuming the Cube is ~38 mm square, centred on the holes; please check).
  - **F1** (0451 fuse) moves, for example to the top next to D1 or to the bottom just outside the body.
  - Small moves: **U15** (+1.2 mm in x), **R90**, **D15** (0.3 mm).
- Top side: **D1** (SMCJ33A) clashes with the new pad positions. It moves behind C112, to about y 132..138.4.
  J14 keeps 0.7 mm to the GND pad and J3 just clears the slot.
- Power copper: the GND (left) / VBATT (right) split around the connector stays. Redraw the local
  F/B pours and stitching vias for the new pad positions. Keep the pad to inner VBATT plane connection at least as
  strong as now (many vias). The back ESC VBATT feeds (F.Cu zones at y 125..140) need a re-check.
- The bottom is still not flat because the XT90 body (10.2 mm) stays there, but C112 is no longer on the bottom.

**W + A gives a 94 x 90 mm board with nothing sticking out, so the frame envelope goes from 100 x 102 to 94 x 90.**

### B: close the mid band as well (stretch: high effort, 1-2 days, medium-high risk, only about 3-4 mm more)
- Move the five LED GH connectors (J16; J15/J7/J6/J4) and their 0603 parts out of y 91..99. There is no
  free spot nearby on the top side. Options: the bottom side, combining them into one 6-pin connector, or
  free space after compressing the centre front. Re-route the 26 tracks that cross the band (mostly J1 fan-out to the side connectors) and the inner pours.
  Move the two back ESC blocks forward together (move with tracks) so the four ESCs stay identical.
- **The limit is the centre column, not the ESCs.** With the XT90 flush, the centre needs 29 + 30 + ~27 = ~87 mm. To get
  down to ~83-84 mm, the 5.2 V buck/USB/LDO block at the centre front also has to be compressed by 3-4 mm so the
  Cube can move forward. That means reworking the buck layout and the USB power mux area.
- The reserved "SD card and usb connector" space goes away.
- Result: about **94 x 84-87 mm**. I'd only do this if W+A is still too long.

Rejected: closing the mid band but leaving the XT90 overhanging. That gives a board ~83 deep, but the frame
would still be ~95 mm because C112 has to leave the centre column anyway. That is worse than A for more work.

## C112 on top, and the XT90 back on top later
- C112 on top fits in plan A, behind the Cube (check its height against whatever sits above the board at the back).
- **XT90 on top**: the earlier problem was that the male XT90PW-M on top puts + (the flat side of the
  housing) on the GND pad, and no mirrored part exists. So putting it back on top means **swapping the pad nets**
  (P = VBATT on the left pad seen from the top). The whole centre-back GND/VBATT split then mirrors:
  the local pours, C112 orientation, D1 orientation and the F1 path all change. The body then takes the top
  area x 134..156, y 114..140, so **C112/D1/F1 go back to the bottom**, where the space under the old body is
  free. J14 (x to 136.4) has to move and J3 needs a small nudge.
- If you think XT90-on-top is likely, decide it **before** plan A so the centre-back block is laid out only once.
  Plan A works with either side by mirroring that block; it is the same amount of work.

## Suggested order
1. W + A together, in one change: re-pour, DRC, update the STEP, then check the mechanical fit against the frame.
2. Only if it's still too long: B.

## Result: W + A done (XT90 stays on the bottom)

Board is now **94 x 90 mm** (x 99..193, y 50..140) with nothing sticking out, down from 100 x 102 mm
including the XT90. Renders: `review/v0.11-shrink/` (before/after, top/bottom). STEP: `fab/v0.11-wip/`.
The edits were made by script (`review/v0.11-shrink/shrink.py`, applied to commit 2752312) and then refilled.
**DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 schematic parity issues.** Warnings are no worse than before
(isolated copper 10 -> 6; silk warnings about the same).

What changed:
- **Outline / holes:** edges at x 99 and 193. Corner holes 3.0 mm at (101.5, 52.5) (190.5, 52.5) (101.5, 137.5)
  (190.5, 137.5), an **89 x 85 pattern**. Cube 30x30 holes unchanged. 78 GND/VBATT stitching vias that were
  outside the new edge were removed, plus a few at the new holes.
- **XT90 J18:** moved 12 mm forward to (145, 126). It is still on the bottom. Pads at y 117.1, slots at y 126, and the body
  ends at the back edge.
- **C112** (330 uF): moved to the **top**, at (145, 127.9), behind the XT90 pads. GND pad is on the left, matching the pour split.
- **D1** (SMCJ33A TVS): turned vertical and placed on top **between the two XT90 pads** at (145, 117.5),
  with the GND pad towards the Cube. This puts the TVS as close to the connector as it can be.
- **F1:** moved to the bottom at (146.5, 111.9), into the space C112 left. The 2.25 mm track to C111 is re-routed and shorter.
- The GND/VBATT connector pours (F+B) are reshaped around D1/F1. There are new 3x3 GND and VBATT stitching vias where the
  old XT90 pads were.
- **Parts that would have been under the XT90 body:**
  - U15 (CAN2 transceiver) moved +1.4 mm, with its local fan-out re-routed. R90 and C83 were re-placed next to it.
  - R117 turned vertical next to U19. The CAN_H_2/CAN_L_2 vias next to it were moved.
  - D15 moved 0.25 mm.
- **CAN_H_2 on In2** used to run straight under the new XT90 pads. It now drops to y 120.4.
- **VBATT feed to the ESCs:** the side VBATT strips (F/In2/In3) lost 3 mm of width to the narrower outline, so I
  added In2/In3 VBATT pours at x 105..108 and 182..185. The strips are back to roughly their old width.
  The left ESC3 VBATT pour had been fed by edge vias, so it now has 6 vias under RSHUNT5.
- The J16 "LED" label was moved inside the edge, and the dimensions were updated.

Things to check:
- **C112 on top:** the can (10.5 mm tall, courtyard from y 122.4) starts 12.4 mm behind the rear Cube holes (y 110). Check it against the
  Cube body and anything above the board.
- **XT90 3D placeholder** is a 24.5 x 25.4 x 10.2 box, wider than the footprint courtyard (21.3). In the STEP/3D view it
  overlaps U15/R117 visually. The real XT90PW-M width should be confirmed and the box resized to match.
- The VBATT path from the XT90 to the back ESCs is ~12 mm longer through the connector pours (same width, 2 layers).
- Visual review in KiCad, especially the hand-routed U15 area (x 153..161, y 112..121) and R117/U19.
